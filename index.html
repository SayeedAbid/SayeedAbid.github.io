About

From enterprise document pipelines to computer vision research.

I'm an AI/ML Software Engineer building production-oriented Python, LLM, and RAG systems using LangChain, Hugging Face, PyTorch, vector databases, Docker, and Linux. My focus is reliability: designing AI pipelines with semantic retrieval, prompt orchestration, structured-output validation, error handling, and fallback mechanisms that keep working when the input doesn't cooperate.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of research rigor and production discipline — debugging ML workflows, analyzing failure patterns, and translating technical findings into solutions that engineering and business stakeholders can actually ship. That includes an MSc at Concordia University focused on machine learning and computer vision, alongside applied work in enterprise document processing and financial fraud analytics.
